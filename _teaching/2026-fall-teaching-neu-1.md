Instructor, Northeastern University, undergraduate course, CIVE 2340: Geotechnical Engineering. Spring 2027.

Focuses on the formation, composition, and classification of soil for engineering purposes; soil-water phase relations; water in soil; seepage; stresses in soil; consolidation theory; strength properties of soils; and the basics of geoenvironmental engineering.
